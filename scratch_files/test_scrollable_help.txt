Scrollable Help Menu Test
==========================

The help menu (ctrl-/) now has these improvements:

1. SCROLLABLE PAGER
   - Navigate with arrow keys (↑↓) or vim keys (j/k)
   - Page Up/Page Down for faster scrolling
   - Home/End to jump to top/bottom
   - Quit with 'q' or ESC
   - Shows scroll position at bottom

2. NEWLY ADDED KEYBINDINGS
   - tab/shift-tab for tab switching (was missing!)
   - alt-0 for jumping to tab 10
   - esc for clearing selection
   - ctrl-t for transpose characters
   - alt-shift-' for removing brackets/quotes
   - Better organization of special characters bindings

3. COMPLETE SECTIONS
   All sections now have their full keybindings:
   - NAVIGATION (11 bindings)
   - SELECTION (6 bindings)
   - EDITING (11 bindings)
   - CLIPBOARD (3 bindings)
   - LINES (2 bindings)
   - SEARCH & REPLACE (7 bindings)
   - MULTIPLE CURSORS (3 bindings)
   - SPECIAL (5 bindings)
   - TABS (7 bindings)
   - PANES (6 bindings)
   - GIT (9 bindings in fuss mode)
   - FILE (3 bindings)

Try it now:
1. Press ctrl-/ to open help
2. Use j/k or arrows to scroll line by line
3. Use PageDown to scroll faster
4. Press q or ESC to exit

On smaller terminals, you can now see ALL keybindings
by scrolling, instead of having them cut off!
