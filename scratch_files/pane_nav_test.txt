Navigation Keys (two options):
Standard:
- Ctrl-Shift-Left: Navigate to pane on the left
- Ctrl-Shift-Right: Navigate to pane on the right
- Ctrl-Shift-Up: Navigate to pane above
- Ctrl-Shift-Down: Navigate to pane below

Vim-style (alternate):
- Alt-H: Navigate to pane on the left
- Alt-L: Navigate to pane on the right
- Alt-K: Navigate to pane above
- Alt-J: Navigate to pane below
